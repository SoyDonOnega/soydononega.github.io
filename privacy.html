Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Who uses it

Only the owner of this site, with their own Google account.

What it reads

The Google Drive folders that hold the owner's own notes. Files go straight between the owner's device and Google Drive.

What it keeps

Nothing. There is no server, no database, no analytics and no third party. The sign-in token stays on the owner's device.

How to stop it

Remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
